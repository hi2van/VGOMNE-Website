Core Values
& Beliefs

We are a part of the Christian Missionary Alliance, focusing on discipling and missionary work to bring people to Jesus.
Our mission statement: All of Jesus for All the World.

Biblical Alignment

We believe that the Bible is God-breathed
and is 100% truth.

Salvation in Jesus

We believe that Jesus is God, came in the form of a human as atonement for our sin. He rose, and we are saved when we believe in Him.

Trinty

We believe in the Trinity, that God exists in three persons: Father, Son, Holy Spirit.

Questions?

We welcome everyone to reach out and ask questions that they may have, whether it be philosophical, biblical, or beyond.
Please see our contact form to be connected with someone. If you have any prayer requests, please do not hesistate to
let any member know.
